Google Drive backup

Back up Apple Health data to Google Drive

Direct answer: VitaSync Backup can export Apple Health data from your iPhone and upload the generated files to your own Google Drive account after you authorize it.

Why use Google Drive

A cloud copy can help protect important personal records if you change devices, clean up local files, or want your export archive available outside your iPhone. VitaSync Backup is designed around user-controlled destinations rather than a developer-operated health data server.

Steps

  1. Install VitaSync Backup from the App Store.
  2. Authorize the Apple Health data categories you want to export.
  3. Connect your Google Drive account in the app.
  4. Choose a destination folder when available.
  5. Create an export and upload it to Google Drive.

Files you can keep

VitaSync Backup can create CSV, JSON, Markdown, or combined files. This gives you a readable backup and a format that can be opened later by spreadsheets or other tools.

Privacy note

Google Drive upload is optional. If you connect it, files are uploaded to the Google Drive account you authorize. VitaSync Backup does not sell your health data and does not store your health export files on its own server.
