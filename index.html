Setup guide

How to export Apple Health data with VitaSync Backup.

  1. 1

    Install the iPhone app

    Download VitaSync Backup from the App Store and open it on your iPhone.

  2. 2

    Authorize Apple Health access

    Allow the health, activity, workout, and sleep data types you want VitaSync to read. You can later manage those permissions in Apple Health.

  3. 3

    Select your export

    Choose a date range, data scope, and file format. CSV works well for spreadsheets, while JSON is useful for structured archives.

  4. 4

    Save, share, or upload

    Save files locally, use the iOS share sheet, or connect Google Drive to upload exports to a folder you control.

Privacy position

Your exports stay under your control.

VitaSync Backup reads only the Apple Health data types you authorize. Export files are created on your device and sent only to destinations you choose, such as local storage, the iOS share sheet, or your Google Drive account.

FAQ

Questions people ask before exporting health data.

What is VitaSync Backup?

VitaSync Backup is an iPhone app that exports authorized Apple Health data to local files or Google Drive for personal backup and review.

Can it export Apple Health data to CSV?

Yes. VitaSync Backup can create CSV files, along with JSON, Markdown, and combined export formats.

Does VitaSync modify Apple Health data?

No. VitaSync Backup is designed to read authorized Apple Health data and create export files. It is not designed to write or change your Apple Health data.

Does VitaSync store my health export files on its own server?

No. VitaSync Backup does not operate its own server for storing your health export files. Files are generated on your device and uploaded only to the destination you choose.

Can I use it for medical decisions?

No. VitaSync Backup is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or health recommendations.
