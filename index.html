VitaSync Backup

VitaSync Backup is an iOS app for exporting and backing up selected Apple Health, workout, and sleep data.

The app helps users create personal health export files in JSON, CSV, Markdown, and combined formats. Users choose which Apple Health data types to authorize, where export files are saved, and whether to connect Google Drive for cloud backup.

VitaSync Backup can save export files locally on the user's device or upload files to the user's own Google Drive account after Google authorization. The app does not operate its own server for storing health export files.

What VitaSync Backup does

  • Reads only Apple Health data types the user authorizes.
  • Exports selected health, activity, workout, and sleep records.
  • Creates JSON, CSV, Markdown, and combined export files.
  • Saves files locally or uploads them to the user's Google Drive account.
  • Does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
