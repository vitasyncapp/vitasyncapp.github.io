CSV export

Export Apple Health data to CSV

Direct answer: VitaSync Backup can export selected Apple Health data to CSV so you can open your records in spreadsheet tools or keep a readable personal archive.

Why CSV helps

CSV is one of the easiest formats to inspect. It works with spreadsheet apps, database imports, personal dashboards, and many analysis workflows. For people who want a practical Apple Health backup, CSV is often the first useful format.

How to create a CSV export

  1. Open VitaSync Backup on your iPhone.
  2. Authorize the Apple Health categories you want to export.
  3. Choose your date range and health data scope.
  4. Select CSV as the export format.
  5. Save, share, or upload the exported CSV file.

CSV vs JSON

CSV is best when you want a table that opens easily in spreadsheets. JSON is better when you need a structured archive for software tools or custom analysis. VitaSync Backup supports both formats so you can choose the file type that fits your workflow.

Privacy note

VitaSync Backup reads only the Apple Health data types you authorize. Export files are created on your device and sent only to the destinations you choose, such as local storage, iOS sharing, or your Google Drive account.
